Simple district-level quality checks: do all districts report deaths, and which districts look high/low after matching deaths to MYPES population?
Published

June 17, 2026

1 Objective

This page keeps the checks deliberately simple:

  1. plot district death counts,
  2. confirm district coverage (which districts have zero deaths),
  3. match deaths to district population (MYPES),
  4. map crude death rates to flag potential over- or under-reporting areas.

No modelling or causal claims are made.

9 Interpretation notes

  • These are descriptive QA checks only.
  • Districts with zero deaths or extreme crude rates should be reviewed for completeness/coding issues before interpreting as true epidemiological differences.
  • Extreme high/low districts can reflect real variation, denominator mismatch, or under/over-reporting; this page is intended to flag where to investigate further.
