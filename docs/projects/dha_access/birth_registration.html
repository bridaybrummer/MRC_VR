Published

January 20, 2026

1 Scope

Most births in South Africa occur in health facilities, and a subset of public hospitals host DHA satellite desks where birth registration is performed on-site. Birth registration coverage should therefore be computed against the combined set of registration nodes:

  • 315 standalone DHA offices
  • 416 hospital-based DHA satellites (curated list linked to MFL coordinates via DHA_access/link_DHA_to_MFL.r)

All numbers use Method 3 — ward centroid to nearest registration node — applied to this augmented node set.

2 Headline coverage

Important
  • 41.3% of the population lives within 5 km of a registration node
  • 65.4% of the population lives within 10 km of a registration node
  • 85.9% of the population lives within 20 km of a registration node

8 Notes on the hospital DHA linkage

The hospital-list-to-MFL match (see DHA_access/dha_mfl_matched.csv) is fuzzy and includes weak matches flagged for manual review. Coverage figures should be interpreted as the upper bound under the current linkage — manual curation of the Weak — verify matches and follow-up on unmatched private facilities will refine the estimate.

9 Limitations

Warning
  • Service hours: hospital DHA desks may have limited operating hours; we assume full availability.
  • Linkage quality: ~9% of matches are flagged “Weak — verify”. See dha_mfl_unmatched.csv and dha_mfl_matched.csv for the audit trail.
  • All Method 3 limitations apply — centroid proxy, straight-line distance, static population. See Methodology.

For methodology, see Methodology. For a check that DHA offices are in plausibly dense locations, see the Sensitivity check. For alternative methods, see Methods Comparison.
