1 Data sources

Source Description Years available
NMC surveillance Notifiable Medical Conditions master database — all notifications, with patient vital status (Alive / Deceased / Unknown) 2016 – 2026
MACOD (LGH) South African vital registration (Medical Certificate of Cause of Death) underlying-cause ICD-10 codes, filtered to NMC-mapped codes 2016 – 2022

ICD-10 codes for each NMC condition are sourced from the verified SA NMC ICD Reference (v3, June 2026). Matching to MACOD uses the 3-character ICD-10 prefix (e.g. A15 covers all TB pulmonary subtypes). Run nmc_macod_wrangling.R to rebuild these tables from source.

2 NMC Surveillance — Annual Notifications

Total NMC case notifications per condition per year (duplicates excluded). The Deceased sub-row shows cases where patient vital status was recorded as Deceased at point of notification.

3 NMC Surveillance — Deaths (Deceased Cases)

Cases where patient_vital_status == "Deceased" at notification. This reflects deaths captured within the NMC surveillance system and is likely an undercount of true condition-specific mortality.

4 MACOD (Vital Registration) — Deaths Coded to NMC ICD-10 Groups

Deaths in the South African vital registration system where the underlying cause of death was coded to an ICD-10 code that maps to a notifiable medical condition. Matching uses the 3-character ICD-10 prefix. Conditions that share a 3-character prefix are grouped together (e.g. U07 covers COVID-19 and Respiratory disease — novel pathogen).

5 Summary Totals

Note

MACOD counts include all deaths with a matching ICD-10 underlying cause, not only cases that were formally notified to the NMC system. The 2020–2021 spike in MACOD deaths reflects COVID-19 (U07.1) mortality — now mapped to the NMC as Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) and Respiratory disease — novel pathogen.

6 Sensitivity of MACOD in Detecting NMC Deaths

This section estimates how well vital registration (MACOD) captures deaths from notifiable medical conditions, using NMC surveillance deaths as the reference standard.

\[\text{Capture ratio (\%)} = \frac{\text{MACOD deaths (vital registration)}}{\text{NMC-notified deaths (surveillance)}} \times 100\]

The join uses the 3-character ICD-10 prefix on both sides, restricted to overlapping years 2016–2022. NMC deaths are cases where patient_vital_status == "Deceased" was recorded in the surveillance system.

Important

Interpretation: A capture ratio > 100 % means MACOD recorded more deaths than NMC surveillance flagged as deceased — expected because NMC death recording is incomplete. A ratio < 100 % indicates MACOD may be undercoding that condition (e.g. vague underlying-cause coding, miscoding to a non-NMC ICD). NA indicates no NMC deaths were recorded for that condition in the reference period.
